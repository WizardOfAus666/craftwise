# CraftWise

**Author:** Gaige

**CraftWise** is an Old School RuneScape (OSRS) RuneLite plugin for Crafting profit analysis, break-even calculations, live Grand Exchange opportunity tracking, bank-aware crafting planning, and level-to-99 route optimization.

## Features

- **Home & Session Tracker**
  - Displays your live Crafting level, XP, progress to next level, and XP remaining to your goal (with optional manual level mode).
  - Highlights top unlocked methods for GP/hr, XP/hr, cheapest training, and balanced efficiency, alongside saved goals and a Crafting session P/L tracker.
- **Recipes & Full Breakdown**
  - Browse verified OSRS Crafting methods across Gem Cutting, Jewellery, Leather, Dragonhide, Glassblowing, Battlestaves, Pottery, Silver Crafting, Spinning, Weaving, and more.
  - Inspect full ingredient costs, expected variable/crush outputs (e.g. Opal, Jade, Red Topaz), 2% GE tax breakdown, level/quest/tool requirements, and break-even analysis.
- **Planner, Calculator & Price Charts**
  - Build single-method or multi-stage training routes (cheapest, fastest XP, balanced, or profit-focused) within a set GP budget, complete with 4-hour GE buy-limit and inventory estimates.
  - Calculate custom quantities, custom buy/sell prices, break-even sell prices, side-by-side method comparisons, and historical margin charts.
- **Market Watcher**
  - Scans verified recipes using OSRS Wiki `/latest`, `/mapping`, `/5m`, and `/1h` data to highlight profitable, newly profitable, improving, and near break-even methods.
  - Track favourite methods in a custom Watchlist with configurable profit and break-even alerts.
- **Bank-Aware Crafting**
  - Scans your bank and inventory (separating unnoted vs. noted items, or optionally including noted items) to show ready-to-craft methods, owned material value, bank-assisted training routes, and an itemized shopping list.

## Price & Game Data Sources

- Live prices and volume data are retrieved from the OSRS Wiki Real-time Prices API (`prices.runescape.wiki`).
- Recipe metadata and item mechanics follow Old School RuneScape / OSRS Wiki data (`oldschool.runescape.wiki`).

## Disclaimer

Market prices are sourced dynamically from community trades reported by the OSRS Wiki Prices API and should be treated as live estimates rather than guaranteed Grand Exchange execution prices.
